Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

SiteTrace SEO is an independent project operated by Edy Appenzeller. This policy covers seo.sitetrace.it.com and the SiteTrace SEO audit tool. It is short because the service is small and deliberately collects very little.

The short version. The audit runs in your browser, not on our servers. The report PDF is generated on your device. We store your licence key and a usage counter, and nothing else. We do not sell data, and we do not run advertising trackers on this tool.

1. What we collect

Only what the service needs to function:

  • Your licence key and plan. If you subscribe, we receive your email address and a licence key from our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy. We store the key, your plan tier, and a running count of audits used against your daily quota.
  • Audit targets. When you run an audit we relay one request to the URL you asked about, so the page can be fetched and analysed in your browser. We do not keep a copy of the fetched HTML and we do not store the URL you audited.
  • Technical logs. Our host, Cloudflare, records standard request data (truncated IP address, user agent, referrer, requested path) for security and abuse prevention.
  • Aggregate analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-first and does not use cookies or build a profile of you across sites.

We do not ask for, and do not want, your name, your company, or a list of the sites you audit.

2. Where your data is processed

The audit itself runs entirely in your browser. The page HTML is fetched by our relay, handed straight to your browser, and parsed on your device. The checks, the score and the PDF are all produced locally — the raw HTML is never written to disk.

Licence keys and usage counters are stored in Cloudflare KV, a key-value store operated by Cloudflare. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, who acts as the merchant of record: they handle billing, receipts and sales tax. We receive the licence key and plan tier; we never receive or store your full card details.

3. Cookies and local storage

We set no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking cookies. The service stores your licence key, your chosen plan, and your branding preferences (logo, business name, accent colour) in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in and your reports keep your branding. That data stays on your device unless you clear it.

4. Third-party services

When you run an audit, we relay a request to the URL you supplied. Those sites see the request; we do not control what they do with it. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of sites you audit. Our own links out, such as to the payment processor or to documentation, are subject to those providers' policies.

5. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will respond within 30 days. Since most of what we hold is a licence key and a counter, this is usually a matter of deleting the key, which also ends your access.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or another jurisdiction with equivalent rights, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Retention

Licence keys and usage counters are kept for as long as your subscription is active and for 30 days afterwards. Technical logs follow Cloudflare's standard retention schedule. We keep no database of visitor identities.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Audit targets are fetched by a relay that refuses to contact private or internal network addresses, so it cannot be used to probe infrastructure behind a firewall. No system is perfect, but we do not hold anything we would be upset to lose.

8. Children's privacy

This is a technical utility for web professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be announced in the product itself.
