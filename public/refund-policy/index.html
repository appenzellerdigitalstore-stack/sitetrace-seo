Last updated: 24 October 2026

SiteTrace SEO is operated by Edy Appenzeller. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, who act as the merchant of record and issue the receipt. This policy explains when a refund is available and how to request one.

30 days, no questions asked. If SiteTrace SEO is not useful to you, tell us within 30 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full.

1. The 30-day money-back window

Every customer can request a full refund within 30 days of their first payment. You do not need to explain why, and you do not need to stop using the service first.

Send the email address you used at checkout. We will process the refund to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement.

2. Annual plans

If you bought an annual plan, the same 30-day window applies. After 30 days we will not normally refund a partial annual term, but we will refund the unused portion if you cancel for a qualifying reason such as the service being unavailable or materially different from what was advertised.

3. Cancelling

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time using the manage-subscription link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt, or by emailing us. Your access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, and no further charges are made.

4. When a refund may be declined

We reserve the right to decline a refund where a licence has been abused — for example, automated high-volume auditing, attempted access to private or internal network addresses, or a claim made after the 30-day window without a qualifying reason.

5. How to request a refund

  • Email support [at] sitetrace.it.com from the address you used to buy.
  • Include the email address on the purchase. We can then find the matching order.
  • State that you are requesting a refund under the 30-day window.

We aim to acknowledge refund requests within two business days. Refunds are processed by Lemon Squeezy on our instruction.

6. Disputes

If you think a refund was wrongly declined, reply to the email and we will look again. If we still cannot agree, you can use the dispute process offered by your bank or card issuer.
